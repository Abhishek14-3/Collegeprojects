1. WHAT IS MY PROJECT?
================================================================================

Wander Wallet is an end-to-end, group travel planning and financial workspace
engineered to resolve the most stressful parts of traveling together:
budget uncertainty, unverified pricing, chaotic shared expenses, and unfair
debt settlements.

Unlike generic budgeting apps (like Splitwise or Excel) that only track raw 
numbers after the money is already spent, and unlike generic travel sites 
(like TripAdvisor or MakeMyTrip) that focus on individual bookings, Wander Wallet
combines the entire lifecycle of group travel into one coherent workspace:

  1. Collaborative Planning & Research:
     - Real-world POIs, transit hubs, and accommodations using open geographic 
       and transit databases (OpenStreetMap, Nominatim, Overpass, Amadeus).
     - Strict price safety: If an exact live price cannot be verified, it is
       explicitly marked as PRICE_UNAVAILABLE. No synthetic or hallucinated
       numbers are ever presented to travelers.

  2. Deterministic Minor-Unit Budget Reality Check:
     - Budgeting engine operating in integer minor units (paise/cents) to 
       guarantee zero floating-point rounding errors (no lost cents).
     - Ratios allocated deterministically across Lodging (31%), Transit (20%),
       Dining (18%), Activities (14%), Local Travel (9%), and Safety Buffer (8%).

  3. Dynamic Multi-Day Itinerary Engine:
     - Supports any duration from 1-day express trips to 14+ day expeditions.
     - Automatically generates morning, afternoon, evening, and night schedules
       clustered around authentic local themes with verified durations.

  4. Group Members & Contributions Workspace:
     - Tracks each traveler's role (Owner, Organizer, Member, Viewer).
     - Differentiates between:
       * Planned Contribution: What a member pledged to pool.
       * Actual Contribution: What they have physically paid into the pool.
       * Planned Share: Their expected financial responsibility.
       * Actual Share: Their real share of expenses consumed.
       * Current Net Balance: Exactly who is owed money vs. who owes money.

  5. Deterministic Expense Engine (4 Split Methods):
     - Equal Split: Remainder paise are absorbed without rounding drift.
     - Exact Amount Split: Validates that exact input sums equal total bill.
     - Percentage Split: Validates that inputs sum to exactly 100%.
     - Custom Split: Supports custom amounts or weighted ratios.
     - Personal Expenses: Can tag individual shopping/personal items that do
       not burden other travelers.

  6. Minimum-Cash-Flow Debt Simplification Settlement:
     - Uses a greedy graph algorithm to resolve mutual debts into the fewest
       possible direct payments, eliminating circular and awkward transfers.

  7. Grounded AI Travel Assistant:
     - Answers queries ("Which day is most expensive?", "Can we fit scuba diving?",
       "What can we do tomorrow with ₹4,000?") strictly from verified database
       context and math engines, without generating fictitious prices.

  8. Travel-Journal Keepsake & PDF Export:
     - Generates a print-styled travel summary designed like an editorial travel
       journal rather than an impersonal accounting invoice.

2. HOW IT IS BUILT (TECH STACK & ARCHITECTURE)
================================================================================

Wander Wallet is engineered as a modern, high-performance web application:

A. Core Frontend & Framework:
   - Next.js 16.3.6 (App Router + Turbopack): Server-side rendering, API route
     handlers, and static page generation.
   - React 19: Concurrent features, modern hooks (`use()`, `useState`, `useEffect`).
   - Styling: Curated editorial design system using Tailwind CSS and CSS custom
     properties. Palette:
       * Background: Soft Cream (#F5F0E6)
       * Primary: Deep Forest Green (#123C35)
       * Accent: Terracotta Clay (#C95D3A)
       * Highlights: Warm Gold (#C9A35B) & Soft Sage (#A9B9A5)
       * Typography: Playfair Display / Serif headings with clean Inter body font.

B. Deterministic Financial Core:
   - TypeScript Minor-Unit Arithmetic: All currency calculations in paise/cents 
     using native JavaScript `bigint` and minor-unit integers. Eliminates IEEE 754
     floating-point rounding inaccuracies (e.g. 0.1 + 0.2 !== 0.3).
   - Safe BigInt JSON Serializer (`serializeBigInt`): Converts `bigint` values
     to strings or numbers safely across HTTP boundaries.

C. Mapping & Geocoding:
   - Leaflet.js with OpenStreetMap and CartoDB Voyager tiles.
   - Completely free, open-source, and does NOT require any proprietary API keys.
   - Nominatim Geocoder & Overpass API for POI querying.

D. Real-World Provider Adapters:
   - Currency: Frankfurter Open Exchange API (European Central Bank data) with
     in-memory TTL caching and deterministic minor-unit conversions.
   - Places & POIs: Nominatim OpenStreetMap geocoding + Overpass API.
   - Transit: Amadeus Transit Adapter + OpenStreetMap Railway/Bus station anchor.
   - Lodging: OpenStreetMap Verified Tourism & Hospitality Overpass adapter.

E. Persistence & State:
   - Resilient In-Memory Data Store (`DataStore` in `src/lib/db/store.ts`):
     Pre-seeded with active demo trips (`trip-goa-2026`), 6 group members,
     multi-currency expenses, and notifications.
   - Prisma ORM Schema: 25 production models ready for PostgreSQL.
   - JWT + Bcrypt Session Engine: Secure HTTP-only cookies for authentication.

3. CORE IDEOLOGY & PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

Wander Wallet is founded on five uncompromising principles:

1. Never Fabricate Travel Data (No AI Hallucinations for Money & Availability):
   - Generative AI should explain and reason, but NEVER invent prices, availability,
     flight schedules, or hotel rates.
   - When real-world live provider APIs have unverified rates, the item is tagged
     with PRICE_UNAVAILABLE. The budget engine handles unpriced items safely
     by excluding them from firm totals and warning the traveler.

2. Zero Floating-Point Drift:
   - In group travel, lost pennies lead to lost friendships. All calculations
     are performed in integer minor units (paise/cents).
   - When ₹100 is split among 3 travelers, the division yields 3,334 paise,
     3,333 paise, and 3,333 paise, guaranteeing the sum is ALWAYS exactly 10,000 paise.

3. Complete Source Transparency:
   - Every price, place, and schedule in the application discloses its source:
     e.g., "Source: Frankfurter API", "Source: OpenStreetMap", "Source: Amadeus".

4. Minimum Cash Flow (Eliminate Circular Debts):
   - If Alice owes Bob ₹1,000, and Bob owes Charlie ₹1,000, Bob should not be
     involved in money transfers. Alice pays Charlie ₹1,000 directly.
   - Wander Wallet uses graph debt simplification to minimize total transactions.

5. Editorial Aesthetic over Sterile Dashboard:
   - Travel is emotional and memorable. Wander Wallet avoids looking like
     enterprise accounting software (e.g. QuickBooks). It feels like an
     editorial travel magazine and passport journal.

4. METHODOLOGY & SYSTEM DESIGN
================================================================================

A. Architecture Layers:

   [Client Browser / Mobile PWA]
                 │
                 ▼
   [Next.js 16 App Router UI Pages]
    (/trips, /trips/[id], /explore, /ai-planner, /profile, /summary)
                 │
                 ▼
   [Server Route Handlers / REST APIs]
    (/api/trips, /api/trips/[id]/expenses, /api/trips/[id]/settlement, /api/ocr/scan)
                 │
   ┌─────────────┴────────────────────────┐
   ▼                                      ▼
[Deterministic Engines]          [External Provider Adapters]
 - Budget Engine (Minor Units)    - Frankfurter (Currencies)
 - Expense Split Engine           - Nominatim / Overpass (OSM)
 - Settlement Engine (Min Flow)   - Amadeus (Flights/Rail)
 - Itinerary Generator            - Receipt OCR Parser
   │                                      │
   └─────────────┬────────────────────────┘
                 ▼
   [Resilient Store / Prisma ORM Database]

B. Settlement Algorithm (Greedy Minimum Cash Flow):
   1. For each member i in group:
      Net_Balance[i] = Total_Paid_By_Member[i] - Total_Share_Consumed_By_Member[i]
   2. Separate members into two priority lists:
      - Debtors: Net_Balance < 0 (sorted descending by debt)
      - Creditors: Net_Balance > 0 (sorted descending by credit)
   3. Greedily match the largest debtor with the largest creditor:
      Transfer_Amount = min(abs(debtor.balance), creditor.balance)
      Create single direct transaction: Debtor -> Creditor
      Update balances until all balances reach 0.
   4. Result: Circular debts are completely eliminated, minimizing transactions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUESTION 1: FOR THE MAP, DO WE NEED ANY API KEY TO USE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER:
NO. You do NOT need any API key!

How it works:
- Wander Wallet uses Leaflet.js with CartoDB Voyager and OpenStreetMap tiles.
- CartoDB and OpenStreetMap provide free, open-access tile servers that require
  zero authentication tokens, zero credit cards, and zero API keys.
- For geocoding and places of interest (POIs), the application uses OpenStreetMap's
  Nominatim service and the Overpass API, which are also 100% free and open-source.
- If in the future you wish to use Google Maps or Mapbox for custom satellite views,
  the provider adapter architecture is already in place to accept a key, but for
  standard operation, Wander Wallet works completely free out-of-the-box.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUESTION 2: CAN WE ADD EXTRA EXPENSES ON THE TRIP (SHOPPING, ACTIVITIES) AND 
SPLIT ONLY BETWEEN THE SPECIFIC PEOPLE WHO BOUGHT THEM?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER:
YES! This feature is ALREADY fully implemented and working in Wander Wallet!

How it works in Wander Wallet:
1. Selective Participant Splitting:
   - When adding an expense (e.g. "Scuba Diving Gear" or "Shopping at Night Market"),
     the user selects the payer (who swiped the card).
   - Under "Participants", you do NOT have to select everyone! You can check ONLY
     the 2 or 3 people who actually participated.
   - The engine splits the cost ONLY among those selected participants.
   - The travelers who were not selected owe ₹0 and are completely unaffected.

2. Personal Expense Support:
   - If Person A buys clothes, souvenirs, or personal medicine that belongs
     strictly to them, they check the "Personal expense" toggle.
   - The expense is recorded under the trip's total spend and appears in Person A's
     individual dashboard, but it is EXCLUDED from group debt settlement.
   - Nobody else is charged a single rupee.

3. Custom / Exact Amounts:
   - If two people buy things together with different amounts (e.g. Person A bought
     ₹1,500 worth of items and Person B bought ₹500 worth of items on one bill of ₹2,000),
     you select "EXACT" or "CUSTOM" split.
   - You type ₹1,500 for Person A and ₹500 for Person B.
   - The server validates that ₹1,500 + ₹500 === ₹2,000 and assigns the debt exactly.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUESTION 3: RESTAURANT BILL SPLITTING (VEG VS. NON-VEG / DRINKERS VS. NON-DRINKERS)
& OCR RECEIPT SPLITTING METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ANSWER:
This is the single most common cause of arguments on group vacations:
- 4 vegetarians who ordered Dal & Roti (₹300 each)
- 2 non-vegetarians who ordered Prawns & Mutton (₹900 each)
- 3 people who had alcohol (₹1,500 in cocktails)
- 3 people who drank only water/soda.
Splitting the bill equally means the vegetarians and non-drinkers end up
subsidizing the seafood and cocktails!

HERE IS OUR METHODOLOGY & IMPLEMENTATION APPROACH:

A. The "Itemized Multi-Tag Bill Splitter" Methodology:
   Instead of splitting the grand total, the bill is split at the LINE-ITEM level.

   1. Step 1: Receipt Upload & OCR Parsing:
      - A traveler snaps a photo of the restaurant bill (JPG/PNG/WEBP/PDF).
      - The OCR endpoint (`/api/ocr/scan` in Wander Wallet) extracts line items:
          * Paneer Butter Masala (Qty: 2) -> ₹700
          * Butter Garlic Prawns (Qty: 2) -> ₹1,600
          * Cocktails / Beer (Qty: 4)     -> ₹1,800
          * Garlic Naan (Qty: 6)          -> ₹360
          * Subtotal                       -> ₹4,460
          * 5% GST                         -> ₹223
          * 10% Service Charge             -> ₹446
          * Grand Total                    -> ₹5,129

   2. Step 2: Interactive Participant Tagging (Quick Avatars):
      - In the UI, each dish has simple tag presets or traveler avatar chips:
          * "Veg Dishes" -> Select all vegetarians.
          * "Non-Veg Dishes" -> Select only the meat eaters.
          * "Drinks / Alcohol" -> Select only the drinkers.
          * "Shared Staples" (Rice, Breads, Water) -> Select everyone.

   3. Step 3: Proportional Tax & Service Charge Distribution (Crucial Mathematical Rule):
      - Many apps fail here by splitting tax equally. That is mathematically unfair!
        (A person who ate ₹300 of food should not pay the same tax as someone who consumed ₹1,500).
      - Wander Wallet's algorithm calculates each person's Subtotal Ratio:
          Person_Ratio = Person_Food_Subtotal / Bill_Food_Subtotal
      - Tax, tips, and service charges are multiplied by Person_Ratio using
        deterministic integer minor-unit arithmetic.
      - Remainder paise are absorbed so the sum of individual shares equals the
        exact printed receipt total down to the paise.

   4. Step 4: One-Click Ledger Integration:
      - The user reviews the breakdown on-screen.
      - Clicking "Confirm & Split" automatically generates the debt transactions
        in the trip workspace and updates the settlement ledger.

   - When travelers are in remote beaches or mountains without cellular network,
     expenses can be logged offline in IndexedDB and synchronized seamlessly

   - Allow travelers to log an expense simply by texting a group bot:
