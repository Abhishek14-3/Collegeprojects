Quantum computing leverages fundamental principles of quantum mechanics including superposition, quantum entanglement, and quantum interference to process information fundamentally faster than classical supercomputers. Quantum bits or qubits permit simultaneous execution of complex computational algorithms. Fault-tolerant quantum processors, superconducting circuits, and trapped-ion systems enable breakthrough applications in quantum cryptography, molecular chemistry modeling, combinatorial optimization, and quantum machine learning. Quantum supremacy demonstrations highlight the potential of quantum algorithms in breaking RSA encryption and accelerating material science discovery.
